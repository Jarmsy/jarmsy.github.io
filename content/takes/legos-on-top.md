Legos are the best versatile decoration you can buy. I argue this because of both its versatility, and its accessibility. You can have flowers that will never die and pictures that you can't scratch or stain. If the colors do fade over time, they are even cooler now. This bias might stem from my long childhood love of legos, but I will stand on it.
