Everything is policy. The problems we file under "human nature" — racism, bigotry, crime, homelessness — are mostly outcomes of policy decisions, but we simplify them to free will and shrug. Human nature is defined by the context people live in. We've just gotten so used to our contexts that we fail to imagine an alternative.
